# AI-readiness

`AI readiness` is perhaps the biggest challenge in Data Architecture today – AI systems are only as good as the data they consume.

Organisations are discovering that their existing data architectures were designed for reporting and analytics, not for generative AI agents. Significant re-engineering is often required, with data governance being embedded directly into the architecture, rather than treated as a separate process.

Common issues include:
- poor data quality
- duplicate or inconsistent master data
- missing metadata
- lack of (end-to-end) data lineage – Where did this data come from?
- siloed datasets
