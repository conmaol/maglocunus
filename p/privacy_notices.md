# Privacy notices

A `privacy notice` is a document, generally attached to a website, that explains how the organisation that runs the website collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data about users or visitors.

Privacy notices are sometimes known as ‘privacy policies’.

A good privacy policy explains:
- What data is collected from users?
  - name
  - email address
  - phone number
  - address
  - IP address
  - cookies and tracking data 
- Why this data is collected?
  - providing a service
  - processing payments
  - customer support
  - analytics and performance monitoring
  - marketing communications
- Who this data is shared with?
  - service providers
  - business partners
  - government authorities
- How this data is protected?
  - encryption
  - access controls
  - security monitoring 
- How long the data is retained?
- What rights individuals have?
  - to access their data
  - to have inaccurate data corrected
  - to have their data deleted
  - to object to their data being used for certain purposes.
