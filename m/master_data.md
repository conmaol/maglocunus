# Master data

`Master data` is one of the three main kinds of data processed by organisations. It consists of core business data that is shared and used across multiple systems, processes and departments in the organisation. Master data describes the key entities involved in business activities, and hence tends to change relatively infrequently.

Typical examples of master data entity types are:
- *customers* – customer ID, name, address, contact details
- *products* – product code, description, category, price
- *suppliers* – supplier ID, company name, payment terms
- *employees* – employee ID, role, department

The other two main kinds of data processed by organisation are:
- [transactional data](../t/transactional_data.md) – records of business events, like orders, payments and shipments; updates daily
- [reference data](../r/reference_data.md) – standardised lists of country codes, currencies, status values, etc.

`Master Data Management` (MDM) is the practice of:
- creating a single, authoritative source of master data
- defining ownership and governance
- removing duplicates
- synchronising data across systems.
